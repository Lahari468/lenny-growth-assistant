Lenny: Welcome to the show, Jane. Really excited to have you here today.

Jane: Thanks so much for having me, Lenny. I've been a longtime listener.

Lenny: Let's start at the beginning. How do you personally define product-market fit?

Jane: For me, product-market fit is the moment growth stops feeling like pushing a boulder uphill and starts feeling like the boulder is rolling downhill on its own. Retention curves flatten out instead of decaying to zero, and word of mouth starts doing real work for you.

Lenny: That's a great way to put it. What are some early signals teams should watch for?

Jane: Watch your cohort retention curves closely. If week-four retention is flattening rather than continuing to decay, that's a strong early signal. Also pay attention to how organically people are inviting others in, without you prompting them.

Lenny: Any common mistakes you see founders make while searching for it?

Jane: The biggest one is optimizing for top-of-funnel growth before you have retention. You end up pouring water into a leaky bucket, and it just masks the underlying problem instead of solving it.
