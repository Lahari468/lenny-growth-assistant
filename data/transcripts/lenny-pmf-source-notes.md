# How to know if you've got product-market fit

> Demo knowledge-base fixture: source notes, not a verbatim transcript.

These notes summarize the product-market-fit signals represented by the linked Lenny source.

## Retention as a PMF signal

A useful product-market-fit signal is whether user retention eventually stabilizes instead of continually declining. Cohort retention behavior helps teams evaluate whether users continue to receive value from a product.

## Organic pull

Increasing demand from the market can be another signal. When users actively seek out a product and growth begins to feel more organic, that can indicate stronger product-market fit.

## Segment-specific PMF

Product-market fit does not have to appear across the entire market simultaneously. A product can first find strong fit with a specific customer segment or audience, then use that evidence as a starting point for expansion.

## Practical takeaway

Teams should look beyond top-of-funnel growth. Retention behavior, organic demand, and evidence of strong fit within a particular segment are useful signals when evaluating product-market fit.
